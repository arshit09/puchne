Maintenance

ButtonDoes
Clear HistoryDeletes saved prompts. Settings untouched
Reset AllDefaults everywhere + clears history. Custom tools and overrides go too
Reset All doesn't revoke site permissions. Those are Chrome's — withdraw per tool, or remove the extension.
